- 生成时间：2026-07-17 18:03:00

- 固定配置：`curvature-mode=robust-mean`，`target_faces=input face count`，`iters=5`，`cd_samples=50000`。
- 对比变量：`inverse-sqrt` 对应 `target_h = lambda / sqrt(kappa)`；`inverse-linear` 对应 `target_h = lambda / kappa`。
- 局部指标：在 GT 上计算 robust mean curvature signal，取面积采样点 top 5/10/20% 高曲率区域，报告 GT->output 最近邻距离。数值越小越好。

## Aggregate Summary

This T-series run covers 31 numeric `T*.off` meshes from `examples`. Duplicate `T21.obj` and the much larger `Th1/Th2` meshes were excluded. Each mesh used `curvature-mode=robust-mean`, the same input `target_faces`, `iters=5`, and only changed the `target_h` mapping.

Overall, `inverse-linear` consistently makes the high-curvature target length smaller: top10 high-curvature `target_h` mean decreased by 25.92%, final output face count increased by 6.10% on average, and average triangle quality `qavg` decreased by 2.37%.

| Metric | Mean delta, linear vs sqrt | Improved >1% | Worse >1% | Within 1% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| high-curvature top5 RMSE | -1.90% | 20/31 | 0/31 | 11/31 |
| high-curvature top10 RMSE | -1.44% | 15/31 | 0/31 | 16/31 |
| high-curvature top20 RMSE | -0.84% | 13/31 | 0/31 | 18/31 |
| global symmetric RMSE | +0.84% | 0/31 | 10/31 | 21/31 |

The clearest top10 high-curvature improvements are `T124` (-4.69%), `T5` (-4.24%), `T36` (-3.71%), `T21` (-2.82%), and `T33` (-2.72%). The weakest cases are `T86` (+0.31%), `T53` (+0.25%), and `T8` (+0.04%).

Conclusion: on the T-series, `inverse-linear` is more favorable to high-curvature local fitting, especially top5/top10 regions. The tradeoff is slightly worse global CD, more final faces, and lower average quality. It is a reasonable high-curvature-detail-priority option, but not a default replacement without a follow-up final-face-count-matched experiment.

## 结论

以 high-curvature top10 RMSE 为主指标，`inverse-linear` 在 15 个模型上优于 `inverse-sqrt`，0 个模型上变差，16 个模型差异小于 1%。
如果 `inverse-linear` 的 top5/top10 RMSE 下降但 global CD 上升，说明它确实把预算更偏向尖锐区域，代价是平坦区域/整体误差。
如果 high-curvature RMSE 没有稳定下降，则不开根号不能作为默认策略，只适合作为高曲率细节优先的可选模式。

本次实验还看到一个需要控制的混杂因素：虽然两组使用相同 `target_faces` 输入预算，但 `inverse-linear` 的最终输出面数比 `inverse-sqrt` 高 6.23%-16.16%。因此当前结论应表述为：在相同目标预算配置下，`inverse-linear` 会明显压低高曲率区域的 `target_h`，但没有稳定带来高曲率局部误差下降；若要做最终定论，需要再做一轮“匹配最终面数”的校准实验。
